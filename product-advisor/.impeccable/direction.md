# Product Advisor surface direction

Mode: Operate. The user approved the functional plan and said 开搞 on 2026-10-01.
No pre-existing UI or brand authority. This is a code-led functional catalog surface,
not an approved image comp. Concept seed: d12b2ba3, grounded candidate index 6.

Direction: a selection worksheet with inspectable catalog records. A quiet white
canvas, warm pale sage product fields and deep green actions keep the budget and
facts legible. A left history list and right conversation accompany the factual
product grid. Synthetic product illustrations identify categories without claiming
real photographs. Mobile collapses to one column with full-width product records.

FIRST VIEWPORT: show category, budget, hard filters and a place to express usage.
Do not display catalog facts until actual successful remote calls have supplied them.
After search, price, criteria, unknown facts and source disclosure lead the cards.

Signature interaction: select known product records, request comparison in the same
Session, wait for native approval, and see a factual comparison table. Denial retains
only prior evidence. Changing budget produces a newly verified shortlist; saved
snapshots retain original query conditions.

Quality bar: a complete, readable developer-facing application. Keyboard-visible
focus, correctly labelled controls, long IDs confined to secondary diagnostics,
clear pending/denied/error states, no fake purchase/review claims, no layout overflow.
The remote-execution boundary is a functional constraint, not decorative copy.

Known tradeoffs: local visitor cookie is demo ownership, not production login.
The synthetic product drawings intentionally do not differentiate physical models.
No third-party design reference or approved raster comp was used.
